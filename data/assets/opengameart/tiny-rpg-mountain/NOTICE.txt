Tiny RPG Mountain Tileset by Luis Zuno (@ansimuz) (OpenGameArt.org)
Source page: https://opengameart.org/content/tiny-rpg-mountain-tileset
Source package: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/tiny_rpg_mountain_files.zip
License: public-domain style permission in the bundled public-license.txt
(the OpenGameArt page lists the set as CC0; the bundled public-license.txt
allows use in personal and commercial projects, modification, and re-distribution)

This directory ships the unmodified transparent PNGs actually referenced by the
game data plus the original public-license.txt:
- tileset.png (368x128, 23x8 cells of 16px, RGBA) — cliff faces, stone bricks,
  dirt ground, stone steps, boulders and grass tufts used by the Round 91
  "Cloud Ridge North Terrace - Wild Goose Cliff" map layers and the expanded
  640x448 world overview.
- bridge.png (160x80, 10x5 cells of 16px, RGBA) — wooden plank deck, side rails
  and hanging ropes used by the Round 91 bridge landmark layers.

Aseprite sources and preview images from the download package are NOT shipped.
